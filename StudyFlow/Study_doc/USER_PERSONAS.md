# User Personas – StudyFlow

## Persona 1: College Student

Needs:
- Track assignments
- Plan examination preparation
- Remember submission dates

Pain Points:
- Multiple deadlines
- Difficulty prioritizing tasks
- Unorganized study schedules

## Persona 2: Competitive Exam Aspirant

Needs:
- Organize different subjects
- Track revision tasks
- Monitor daily progress

Pain Points:
- Large syllabus
- Inconsistent planning
- Difficulty tracking completed topics

## Persona 3: Independent Learner

Needs:
- Create personal learning goals
- Monitor progress
- Organize learning activities

Pain Points:
- Lack of structure
- Difficulty maintaining consistency

## Product Opportunity
StudyFlow provides a simple task-management experience that addresses
these common planning challenges.
